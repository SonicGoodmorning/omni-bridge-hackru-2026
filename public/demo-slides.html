Consent guide

One section at a time

  1. Select the source section and participant language
  2. Prepare a simpler explanation with its source attached
  3. Inspect the target text, back-translation, and flags
  4. Release the current draft after coordinator review
The tool explains the document; a human conducts consent05
Demonstrate randomization. Confirm 50% chance, neither person choosing the group, possible placebo, and no guaranteed benefit. Before release, point out that the patient side has not received the draft. The interface does not collect signatures or grant enrollment.

Conversation bridge

Speak. Review the transcript.
Send. Review the explanation.
Then release.

Typed input remains available. Browser recognition may use a remote speech service; synthetic demonstrations only.

Source edits and language changes invalidate earlier drafts06
Show push-to-talk transcription or paste the fixed conversation. Correct recognition before sending. Native speech recognition is browser-dependent and may process audio remotely. Without a key, only the documented fixed sample conversation is supported. Do not silently substitute it for arbitrary speech.
